## ❓ ANTICIPATED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### Q: Why blockchain instead of traditional database?
**A:** "Blockchain provides immutability, transparency, and decentralization. Once a trademark is registered, it cannot be altered or deleted. Anyone can verify ownership without trusting a central authority."

### Q: What about gas fees?
**A:** "We use Polygon, which has significantly lower gas fees than Ethereum mainnet - typically less than $0.01 per transaction. For production, we could implement meta-transactions for gasless user experience."

### Q: How do you prevent fake registrations?
**A:** "We have an admin verification workflow. All registrations go through manual review before being marked as verified. This combines blockchain's immutability with human quality control."

### Q: What about scalability?
**A:** "Polygon can handle thousands of transactions per second. IPFS provides distributed storage that scales horizontally. The architecture is designed for enterprise-level usage."

### Q: Is this production-ready?
**A:** "This is a proof-of-concept on testnet. For production, we'd need: mainnet deployment, legal compliance review, enhanced security audits, and integration with official trademark offices."

### Q: How does the QR code verification work?
**A:** "The QR code contains a link to our verification page with the token ID. When scanned, it queries the blockchain directly to verify authenticity. This works offline-to-online, bridging physical products with digital verification."
