SECTION 14: COMPARISON & ALTERNATIVES
================================================================================

Q63. Why blockchain over traditional database?
ANSWER:
BLOCKCHAIN ADVANTAGES:
- Immutable ownership records
- Transparent and verifiable
- No central authority needed
- Global accessibility
- Trustless transactions
- Censorship resistant

TRADITIONAL DATABASE:
- Centralized control
- Can be modified/deleted
- Requires trust in operator
- Jurisdiction limitations
- Single point of failure

OUR APPROACH: Hybrid - critical data on blockchain, supplementary in database

Q64. Why Polygon over other blockchains?
ANSWER:
COMPARED TO ETHEREUM:
- 1000x cheaper transactions
- Faster confirmation (2s vs 15s)
- Same security model (Ethereum-backed)
- EVM compatible

COMPARED TO SOLANA:
- More mature ecosystem
- Better documentation
- Easier development (Solidity)
- More wallet support

COMPARED TO BSC:
- More decentralized
- Better security reputation
- Ethereum compatibility

Q65. Why IPFS over centralized storage (AWS S3)?
ANSWER:
IPFS ADVANTAGES:
- Decentralized (no single point of failure)
- Content-addressed (immutable)
- Censorship resistant
- Permanent storage (with pinning)
- Aligns with Web3 philosophy

AWS S3:
- Centralized control
- Can be taken down
- Requires ongoing payments
- Not censorship resistant

Q66. Could this work without blockchain?
ANSWER:
TECHNICALLY YES, but loses key benefits:
- No immutable proof of ownership
- Requires trust in central authority
- Can be manipulated or censored
- No transparent verification
- No smart contract automation
- No global accessibility without jurisdiction

BLOCKCHAIN PROVIDES:
- Trustless verification
- Transparent ownership history
- Automated royalty payments
- Global, permissionless access
- Censorship resistance
