# Gas Fees in TrademarkChain - Simple Explanation

## What are Gas Fees? 🤔

Gas fees are like **processing charges** you pay when making a transaction on the blockchain. Similar to:
- Bank transaction fees
- Credit card processing fees
- Payment gateway charges

But MUCH cheaper!

---

## Why Do We Need Gas Fees? 💡

When you register a trademark or make a purchase:
1. Transaction is sent to blockchain network
2. Validators verify and process the transaction
3. Transaction is permanently recorded on blockchain
4. Validators receive gas fees as payment for their work

**Think of it as:** Paying a small fee to notarize a document, but digitally and instantly!

## Frequently Asked Questions ❓

**Q: Do I need to pay gas fees every time?**
A: Yes, for write operations (register, buy, sell). Read operations (verify, browse) are free.

**Q: Can gas fees increase?**
A: Yes, they fluctuate based on network congestion, but Polygon is consistently low (₹0.80-₹4.20 range).

**Q: What if I don't have MATIC?**
A: You can buy small amounts (₹100) from Indian exchanges like WazirX or CoinDCX.

**Q: Are gas fees refundable?**
A: No, they're paid to network validators. But they're so low it's negligible.

**Q: Why not make it completely free?**
A: Gas fees are inherent to blockchain technology. They prevent spam and compensate validators. We've minimized them as much as possible.
